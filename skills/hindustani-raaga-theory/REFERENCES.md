## Notes on Accuracy

- Hindustani classical music is an **oral tradition** — raaga interpretations vary across gharanas
  (schools/lineages). Where interpretations differ, we have followed the most widely accepted
  version based on the Bhatkhande system.
- **Pakad phrases** are representative, not definitive — different artists may render them
  with slight variations, which is accepted in the tradition.
- **Thaat classification** follows Bhatkhande's 10-thaat system. Some raagas (marked with *)
  may be classified under different thaats by other musicologists.
- **Time assignments** (prahar) are traditional guidelines, not strict rules. Bhairavi, for
  example, is traditionally a morning raaga but is universally performed as the closing raaga
  at any time.
