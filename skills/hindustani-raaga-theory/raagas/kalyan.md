# Raag Kalyan (Yaman)
- **Thaat**: Kalyan
- **Jaati**: Sampurna-Sampurna (7-7)
- **Vadi**: Ga (shuddha)
- **Samvadi**: Ni (shuddha)
- **Time**: Evening — 1st prahar of night (6 PM – 9 PM)
- **Rasa/Mood**: Shringara, devotional, serene, grand

Note: Kalyan and Yaman are often considered the same raaga in modern practice.
See [yaman.md](yaman.md) for complete details.

The traditional "pure" Kalyan may differ slightly from Yaman in emphasis:
- **Kalyan**: may give slightly more weight to Dha and higher register
- **Yaman**: The modern concert version with emphasis on 'N R G opening

Both use: S R G M P D N (tivra Ma, all other swaras shuddha)
