# Raag Bhupali (Bhoopali)
- **Thaat**: Kalyan (sometimes classified under Bilawal)
- **Jaati**: Audav-Audav (5-5) — pentatonic
- **Vadi**: Ga (shuddha)
- **Samvadi**: Dha (shuddha)
- **Time**: Evening — 1st prahar of night (6 PM – 9 PM)
- **Rasa/Mood**: Shanta (peace), shringara (romantic), devotional, calm joy
- **Swaras Used**: S R G P D (all shuddha)
- **Varjit**: Ma and Ni are COMPLETELY omitted
- **Aaroh**: S R G P D S'
- **Avaroh**: S' D P G R S
- **Pakad**: G R S D' S, R G P, D P G R S
- **Chalan Rules**:
  - Pure pentatonic — uses only S R G P D
  - Ma and Ni are strictly forbidden
  - Ga is the soul of the raaga — elongate and emphasize it
  - The movement G P D S' is very characteristic
  - Can be very peaceful and devotional
  - Simple structure makes it a favorite for beginners but depth for masters
  - Pa is a strong resting note
- **Similar Raagas**:
  - **Deshkar**: SAME 5 notes (S R G P D) but Dha is vadi (not Ga), making it a morning raaga
  - **Shuddha Kalyan**: Also uses S R G P D but allows occasional N touch
  - **Yaman**: Full Sampurna version — adds Ma(tivra) and Ni to Bhupali's base
  - **Hindol**: Also pentatonic but uses S G M D N (completely different set)
- **Notable Compositions**: "Nirgun" (many bhajans in this raaga)

## Bhupali vs Deshkar — Key Distinction
Both use IDENTICAL swaras: S R G P D. The difference:
- **Bhupali**: Vadi = Ga, evening raaga, emphasizes Ga with meend
- **Deshkar**: Vadi = Dha, morning raaga, emphasizes Dha and upper register
